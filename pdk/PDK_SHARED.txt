Shared PDK_ROOT
===============

Magic and MAX do not use the same on-disk format:

  Magic  →  $PDK_ROOT/<pdk>/libs.tech/magic/*.magicrc  (open_pdks)
  MAX    →  $PDK_ROOT/max/tech/<name>/                 (make_tech)

Both live under one directory so you do not keep two full foundry trees.

  Host:       data/pdks
  Inside Nix FHS:  /mmi-pdks   (PDK_ROOT)
  Designs:    data/workspace → /mmi-home/work

Tapeout mag2gds needs $PDK_ROOT/sky130A/libs.tech/magic/sky130A.magicrc
from the flake-locked mmi-pdks output (seeded into data/pdks on launch).

Tcl paint dump does not need that file; it is not tapeout-quality.
