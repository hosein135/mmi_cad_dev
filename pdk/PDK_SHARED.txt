Shared PDK_ROOT
===============

Magic and MAX do not use the same on-disk format:

  Magic  →  $PDK_ROOT/<pdk>/libs.tech/magic/*.magicrc
  MAX    →  $PDK_ROOT/max/tech/<name>/                 (make_tech)

Both live under one directory so you do not keep two full foundry trees.

  Host:       data/pdks
  Inside Nix FHS:  /mmi-pdks   (PDK_ROOT)
  Designs:    data/workspace → /mmi-home/work

Foundry PDKs are not fetched by ./run.sh. Import them from MAX when you want
(File / Local → Import PDK). They land in data/pdks.

Tapeout mag2gds needs $PDK_ROOT/sky130A/libs.tech/magic/sky130A.magicrc after
you have imported a sky130 tree (or copied a Magic rc into that path).
