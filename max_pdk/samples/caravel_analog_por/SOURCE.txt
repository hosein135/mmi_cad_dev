Caravel analog user-project sample (Magic → MAX)

This folder is a trimmed copy of the analog example from:

  https://github.com/efabless/caravel_user_project_analog
  path: mag/
  license: Apache-2.0 (upstream)

It is the power-on-reset (example_por) used as the analog user-space
example on Efabless/ChipFoundry Caravel (sky130A), plus the leaf
sky130_fd_pr device cells that example_por instantiates.

Top cell for conversion:  example_por
Also present:             user_analog_proj_example  (two POR instances)

Not included (need the full Caravel padframe / PDK stdcell mag views):
  user_analog_project_wrapper.mag
  sky130_fd_sc_hvl__*  (schmittbuf / buf_8 / inv_8 / fill_4)

Those HVL standard-cell instances become empty placeholders in GDS
unless you point the importer at a folder that also contains their .mag
files from open_pdks.

Upstream docs:
  https://github.com/efabless/caravel_user_project_analog
  https://github.com/chipfoundry/caravel
